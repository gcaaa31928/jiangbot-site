Data Deletion Instructions

jiangbot · 資料刪除說明

If your public post was processed by jiangbot

  1. Email me@redhuang.dev with the subject "jiangbot data deletion".
  2. Include the link to your Threads post, or your Threads username.
  3. We will delete all stored data about those posts within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Even without a request, stored post data is deleted automatically 30 days after the post was published.

如果你的公開貼文被 jiangbot 處理過：寄信到 me@redhuang.dev（主旨「jiangbot data deletion」），附上貼文連結或 Threads 帳號名稱，我們會在 7 天內刪除並回信確認。就算沒有提出要求，貼文資料也會在發文 30 天後自動刪除。

If you connected your Threads account to jiangbot

  1. In Threads, open Settings → Account → Website permissions.
  2. Find jiangbot and remove it. Its access token stops working immediately.
  3. Optionally email me@redhuang.dev, and we will delete the stored token and any related data within 7 days.

若你曾授權 jiangbot：到 Threads 的「設定 → 帳號 → 網站權限」移除 jiangbot，授權會立即失效；也可以來信要求我們在 7 天內刪除儲存的 token 與相關資料。
